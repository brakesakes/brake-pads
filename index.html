What is BrakePadGauge?

Our mechanical brake pad wear gauge provides real-time feedback on your vehicle's brake system, helping truckers and fleet operators prevent brake overheating and failure in demanding conditions.

Simple, reliable, and cost-effective. Your safety, in your hands.

The Product

This innovative system uses a mechanical linkage to monitor brake pad wear and translates it into a real-time indicator on your vehicle's dashboard.
